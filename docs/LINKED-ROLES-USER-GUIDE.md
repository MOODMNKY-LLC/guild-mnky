# How to Get Your Verified Guardian Role

**Important**: Linked Roles require manual claiming - they don't auto-assign!

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Connect Bungie Account (User Settings)
1. Open Discord
2. Click the **gear icon** (⚙️) in bottom left → **User Settings**
3. Go to **Connections** tab
4. Click **Bungie.net**
5. Click **Authorize** to connect your account
6. ✅ Your Bungie account is now connected

### Step 2: Claim Linked Role (Server Settings) ⚠️ **REQUIRED**
1. Right-click the **server name** at the top left: **"D2 SHERPA & LFG HUB"**
2. Click **Server Settings**
3. Go to **Linked Roles** tab (in the left sidebar)
4. Find **"Verified Guardian"** role
5. Click **"Connect"** or **"Claim Role"** button
6. Verify your Bungie.net connection (if prompted)
7. ✅ Role should be assigned immediately!

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "I don't see Linked Roles tab"
- Make sure you're looking in **Server Settings** (not User Settings)
- The tab is in the left sidebar under Server Settings
- If still not visible, the role may not be configured yet

### "Connect button is grayed out"
- Make sure you completed Step 1 (connect Bungie account in User Settings)
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting your Bungie account
- Refresh Discord (close and reopen)

### "Role claimed but not showing in server"
- Wait a few seconds for Discord to sync
- Check if role appears in your role list (right-click your name → Roles)
- If still not showing, contact an admin

### "I connected Bungie but can't claim role"
- Make sure you're connected in **User Settings → Connections**
- Then go to **Server Settings → Linked Roles** to claim
- These are two different places!

---

## 🔄 After Claiming the Role

Once you've claimed the role:
1. ✅ You'll see "Verified Guardian" tag next to your name in gated channels
2. ✅ You can now apply to be a Sherpa at `/sherpa/apply`
3. ✅ Your role will sync to the database automatically

## 📱 Mobile Users

**Note**: Linked Roles must be claimed on **desktop Discord**:
- You can connect your Bungie account on mobile
- But you must claim the role on desktop
- After claiming once, you can use mobile normally
